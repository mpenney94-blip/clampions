# League notes for the written columns

Claude reads this file every time it writes a recap, preview or rivalry column. Add to it whenever there's league lore or
real-life news worth a mention. Columns should bring these up now and then, when they fit, not every week.

## Traditions
- Duck hunt: the team that loses the losers bracket (the toilet bowl: lose every game there and you finish last) has to
  dress up as a duck while everyone else in the league shoots them with paintballs. Any team out of the playoff race is a
  duck-hunt candidate, and anyone near the bottom of the standings is fair game for duck jokes.

## Past last-place punishments
The last-place punishment changes every year; the duck hunt is this year's (2026).
- 2025: a 1v1 game night. Shitmoths (then Pandemic of Shitapillars) finished last and had to do it. Everyone agreed it wasn't
  much of a punishment.
- 2024: the 7/7/9 challenge: eat 7 hot dogs and drink 7 beers over a 9-inning baseball game. Shitmoths (then The Shit Abyss)
  finished last again, and he made it look easy.
- 2023: a beer mile. Cave Of The Sleepers (then Cave Of The Broken) finished last, and he made it look easy too.
Good material: Shitmoths as a two-time last-place finisher, punishments that turned out too easy, and whether the duck hunt
finally makes someone suffer.

## Style
- Pronouns: the ValueVolplane manager (the same manager was ValueVictorie in 2023–24 and ValueValor in 2025) goes by
  she/her. Every other manager is a guy: refer to him or his team as "he".
- Puns are always welcome, especially on team names, player names and the duck hunt.
